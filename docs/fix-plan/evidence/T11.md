## Decisions to know
- `nextReportIn` is in MILLISECONDS (60000), not seconds as the follow-up said: the Android `ReportSchedule` reads it as ms and clamps 15 s..1 h. Env `TELEMETRY_REPORT_INTERVAL_MS`.
- `updateWindow` comes from global settings, not per policy (policies have no such column and `policyStore` was not mine). Per-policy windows would need a policies column.
- `executing` timeout = 2 x (5 min, or 15 min for INSTALL_APK/UPDATE_APP/DOWNLOAD_FILE/UPLOAD_FILE/ENCRYPT_DEVICE); the command becomes `failed` ("timed out while executing"), with a `command-failed` event and a `command.timed_out` audit entry. Late acks then get 409.
- Tokens issued before this release have no `jti`: they keep working until they expire (24 h) and are killed by a password change, but cannot be revoked individually.
- WIPE in a batch only works for the device whose id is in `confirm`.
